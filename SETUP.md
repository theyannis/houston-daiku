# Connect Daiku

1. If you don't have a Daiku account yet, create one at
   [daiku.ai/start](https://daiku.ai/start) and sign in to the sites you want
   your agents to use.
2. In Houston, choose **Read-only** or **Read and write** for **Access**, then
   select **Connect with Daiku**.
3. Daiku opens its sign-in page. Approve with your passkey or screen lock, then
   allow Houston to use your browser. Houston returns once you approve.

> Daiku still asks you before anything pays, buys, sends or deletes, whatever
> access you choose here. To change how often it asks, open Daiku's settings.

## Advanced

### Changing access

Switching between **Read-only** and **Read and write** takes effect right away
and doesn't need a new sign-in.

### Troubleshooting

- If agents report that the Daiku sign-in expired, open the connection in
  Houston and select **Connect with Daiku** again.
- If a site asks you to sign in, your agent shows you the browser with
  **Take the wheel**; sign in there and hand it back.
- See [Daiku help](https://daiku.ai/help) for your browser, plan and approvals.
